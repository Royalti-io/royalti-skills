# Contributing

**The most valuable contribution is a correction.** If a claim in one of these skills is wrong,
out of date, or contradicts a primary source, please tell us — that is exactly the failure mode
this catalog is built to avoid.

## How to contribute

**Open an issue.** [github.com/Royalti-io/royalti-skills/issues](https://github.com/Royalti-io/royalti-skills/issues)

We are not accepting pull requests at this time. That is not a judgement about outside
contributors — it is a consequence of how this catalog is reviewed.

### Why issues and not pull requests

Every normative claim here has to cite a primary source and pass an adversarial verification
pass before it merges (see [`ACCURACY-GATE.md`](ACCURACY-GATE.md)). Applying that standard to
incoming pull requests would mean re-verifying every contributed claim against the
specifications, which is slower than writing the material and much slower than acting on a
well-reported correction. Accepting PRs we could not hold to the gate would quietly lower the
bar the whole catalog rests on.

So: send us what is wrong and what the source says. We will do the verification and credit you.

## What makes a good correction

The more of this you can give us, the faster it lands:

1. **Which skill and which claim.** Quote the sentence.
2. **What is wrong with it.** Wrong outright, out of date, or true-but-overgeneralised — the
   third is the most common and the easiest to miss.
3. **The primary source.** A link to the specification, knowledge base article, or operator
   documentation that supports the correction, ideally with the section or page.
4. **Scope, if you know it.** Many claims in this domain are true for one profile, one version
   or one territory and false elsewhere. If that is what is wrong, say which.

"This is wrong" without a source is still worth sending. We would rather re-check something and
find it fine than leave a wrong claim standing.

## What we will not publish

From [`SCRUB-CHECKLIST.md`](SCRUB-CHECKLIST.md), and it applies to contributions too:

- Internal operations detail from any company, including ours
- Partner-confidential material, contract terms, or roster information
- Claims about any product's capabilities
- Normative claims that cannot be cited to a primary source

Please do not send material you are not free to share. If a correction depends on something
under NDA, tell us what is wrong without the confidential part and we will find a public source
or leave the claim out.

## Suggesting a new skill

Open an issue describing the topic and who would use it. The catalog covers process and format
knowledge in music rights and distribution. The practical constraint is citability: if the
subject has no primary sources we can point at, we cannot publish a skill about it under the
accuracy gate.

## Security and disclosure

If you find credentials, personal data, or confidential material that has escaped the scrub
checklist, please report it privately rather than opening a public issue. Email
`security@royalti.io`.
